MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 3 February 2026 between:

(1) Delta Logistics S.A.E., a joint stock company registered in Cairo under commercial registration
no. 184522, with its head office at 12 El Nasr Road, Nasr City, Cairo (the "Client"); and

(2) Nile Cloud Systems LLC, a limited liability company registered in Giza under commercial
registration no. 99317, with its office at 45 Smart Village, Cairo-Alexandria Desert Road, Giza
(the "Provider").

1. SERVICES
1.1 The Provider shall supply the managed fleet-tracking platform, hosting and support services
described in Schedule 1 (the "Services").

2. TERM AND RENEWAL
2.1 This Agreement becomes effective on the first day of the calendar month following the date of
signature (the "Effective Date").
2.2 The initial term is twenty-four (24) months from the Effective Date (the "Initial Term").
2.3 After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month
periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party gives at least ninety (90) days' written notice
of non-renewal before the end of the then-current term.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
3.1 The Client shall pay a service fee of EGP 145,000 (one hundred forty-five thousand Egyptian
pounds) per calendar quarter, invoiced quarterly in advance.
3.2 Fees are exclusive of value added tax, which shall be added at the prevailing rate (currently 14%).
3.3 Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Overdue amounts bear interest at
1.5% per month until paid.
3.4 Fees are fixed for the Initial Term. For each Renewal Term the Provider may increase fees by no
more than 7% by written notice given at least sixty (60) days before the Renewal Term begins.

4. SERVICE LEVELS
4.1 The Provider shall make the platform available at least 99.5% of the time in each calendar month.
4.2 For each full 0.5 percentage point by which monthly availability falls below 99.5%, the Client is
entitled to a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee, up to a maximum of 25% of the monthly fee.
For this purpose the monthly fee is one third of the quarterly fee.

5. TERMINATION
5.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party
commits a material breach that is not remedied within thirty (30) days of written notice of the breach.
5.2 After the first twelve (12) months of the Initial Term, the Client may terminate for convenience
by giving sixty (60) days' written notice and paying an early termination fee equal to two (2)
quarterly fees.

6. LIABILITY
6.1 Each party's total liability under this Agreement in any twelve-month period is limited to the
fees paid or payable by the Client in the twelve (12) months before the event giving rise to the claim.
6.2 The limit in clause 6.1 does not apply to breach of confidentiality or to death or personal injury
caused by negligence.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
7.1 Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret during the term of this
Agreement and for three (3) years after it ends.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Arab Republic of Egypt.
8.2 Any dispute shall be finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Cairo Regional Centre
for International Commercial Arbitration (CRCICA), seated in Cairo, in the English language, before
three arbitrators.

Signed for and on behalf of Nile Cloud Systems LLC: Rania Samir, Managing Director
